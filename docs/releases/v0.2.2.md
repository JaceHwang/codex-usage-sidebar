# Codex Usage Sidebar v0.2.2

Version 0.2.2 makes reset and Bank expiry countdowns faster to scan while preserving the native,
compact Codex popover style.

## Highlights

- Enlarges and strengthens the numeric portions of intervals such as `（5d21h）` and `(2d6h)`.
- Uses the live quota color for interval digits, matching the percentage and quota state.
- Keeps `d`, `h`, `m`, parentheses, and past-time suffixes smaller and muted.
- Preserves one-line reset and Bank dates without adding a capsule, background, border, or extra
  card width.
- Applies the same semantic hierarchy in Codex light and dark themes.
- Preserves Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and English formatting.

## Validation

The release adds compact-duration segmentation regressions for full-width and ASCII parentheses,
future and past intervals, Bank status suffixes, and non-duration values. The release payload is
promoted from the exact successful GitHub Actions artifact and recorded in
`assets/PROVENANCE.json`.
